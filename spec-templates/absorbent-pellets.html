5X Absorbent Pellets

American BioCarbon's renewable absorbent pellets absorb up to 5× their own weight in water, double the capacity of wood-based pellets and roughly five times that of clay. Manufactured from single-source recycled sugarcane bagasse, they ship and store stable, then disintegrate on contact to expose the full internal pore structure.

Plant-Based Advantage

Made from single-source recycled sugarcane bagasse. The cane stalk carries a native vascular structure with open parenchyma cells. Natural lignin binds the pellet during pressing, with no synthetic adhesives in the product or the finished waste.

Freight Mathematics

One 44,000-lb truckload of ABC bagasse absorbs roughly 26,400 gallons of water-based liquid. The same truckload of wood pellet covers 13,200 gallons; of clay, 5,275 gallons.

One truck of bagasse  =  2 trucks of wood pellet  =  5 trucks of clay

Honeycomb Microstructure Holds More

Peer-reviewed studies of bagasse-derived sorbents describe a honeycomb-like internal structure with cavities and channels at the micro and macro scale (Castro et al., 2017). Native wood porosity is dominated by sub-2-nanometer pathways and slow capillary uptake (Plötze et al., 2020). Bagasse moves liquid faster, holds more of it, and breaks down on schedule.
